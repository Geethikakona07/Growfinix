Interview Q&A

Q: How do you make short-form content go viral?

Hook viewers in first 0.5 seconds, use trending audio, create emotional peaks, and include a clear CTA. The algorithm rewards watch time and shares.

Q: What makes CapCut better than other editing tools?

CapCut offers free auto-captions, trending templates, and one-click 9:16 formatting. The AI features save hours of manual editing.

Q: How do you measure success for short-form video?

Primary metrics: watch-through rate (>70%), share rate (>2%), and save rate (>1%). For game launches, also track install attribution and CPA.

Q: How do you repurpose long-form content efficiently?

Use Pictory's AI to auto-detect highlight moments, then CapCut for captioning and branding. A 1-hour podcast becomes 8-12 clips in under 30 minutes.
