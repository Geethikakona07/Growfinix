## Target Audience Personas

### Persona 1: Casual Gamer (Primary)
- **Age:** 18-28
- **Behavior:** Plays 30-60 min/day, scrolls social media 2+ hours
- **Motivation:** Fun, social competition, quick dopamine
- **Platform:** Instagram Reels, TikTok
- **Content Type:** Gameplay highlights, memes, challenges

### Persona 2: Hardcore Gamer (Secondary)
- **Age:** 22-35
- **Behavior:** Plays 2+ hours/day, follows gaming influencers
- **Motivation:** Mastery, rankings, exclusive content
- **Platform:** YouTube Shorts, Twitch clips
- **Content Type:** Skill showcases, strategy tips, pro gameplay

### Persona 3: Social Gamer (Tertiary)
- **Age:** 16-24
- **Behavior:** Plays with friends, shares content daily
- **Motivation:** Social connection, trends, FOMO
- **Platform:** TikTok, Instagram Reels
- **Content Type:** Challenges, duets, user-generated content

## Interview Q&A

**Q: How do you make short-form content go viral?**  
A: Hook viewers in first 0.5 seconds, use trending audio, create emotional peaks, and include a clear CTA. The algorithm rewards watch time and shares, so focus on content people want to rewatch.

**Q: What makes CapCut better than other editing tools?**  
A: CapCut offers free auto-captions, trending templates, and one-click 9:16 formatting. The AI features save hours of manual editing and the template library keeps content feeling fresh.

**Q: How do you measure success for short-form video?**  
A: Primary metrics are watch-through rate (aim >70%), share rate (aim >2%), and save rate (aim >1%). For game launches, also track install attribution and cost per acquisition.

**Q: How do you repurpose long-form content efficiently?**  
A: Use Pictory's AI to auto-detect highlight moments, then CapCut for captioning and branding. This pipeline can turn a 1-hour podcast into 8-12 platform-ready clips in under 30 minutes.
