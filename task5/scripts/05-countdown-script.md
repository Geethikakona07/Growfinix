## Production Notes

### Visual Style
- Countdown timer overlay (big, bold)
- Flash cuts synchronized to beat
- Neon/glowing effects on game logo
- Urgency colors: red, orange, yellow

### Audio
- High-energy electronic music
- Countdown sound effects (beeps)
- Drop/impact on logo reveal

### Caption Text (On-Screen)
```
Frame 1: "TODAY 🚀"
Frame 2: "IT'S HERE"
```

## Urgency Tactics

| Tactic | Implementation | Psychological Trigger |
|--------|----------------|----------------------|
| Timer Overlay | Visual countdown | Loss aversion |

| Exclusivity | "Early players get..." | Status seeking |
