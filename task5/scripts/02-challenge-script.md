# Script 2: The Challenge — "Can You Beat This?"

**Platform:** YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels  
**Duration:** 20-30 seconds  
**Hook Type:** Challenge / Competition  
**Goal:** Drive engagement, encourage UGC  

---

## Script Breakdown

| Timestamp | Visual | Audio/Text | Duration |
|-----------|--------|------------|----------|
| 0:00-0:02 | Player fails spectacularly | "I bet you can't do better 😤" | 2s |
| 0:02-0:05 | "Challenge" text animation | Challenge sound effect | 3s |
| 0:05-0:15 | Gameplay showing the challenge | Excited commentary/music | 10s |
| 0:15-0:20 | Success moment (or epic fail) | "TRY IT!" | 5s |
| 0:20-0:25 | Hashtag overlay + CTA | "Tag someone who can't do this" | 5s |

---

## Production Notes

### Visual Style
- Split screen: left = fail, right = success
- Timer overlay for added tension
- Red/green color coding (fail/success)
- Bold "CHALLENGE ACCEPTED" graphics

### Audio
- Original audio: genuine reaction/excitement
- Sound effect: buzzer for fail, ding for success
- Music: upbeat, competitive energy

### Caption Text (On-Screen)
```
Frame 1: "CAN YOU BEAT THIS?"
Frame 2: "[Challenge Name]"
Frame 3: "#GameChallenge"
Frame 4: "LINK IN BIO 👇"
```
