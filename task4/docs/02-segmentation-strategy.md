## Startup Investment Firm — Audience Segmentation

---

## Segmentation Model

### By Risk Tolerance

| Segment | Description | Email Strategy |
|---------|-------------|----------------|
| **Conservative** | Low risk, capital preservation | Stable income focus, bonds, dividends |
| **Moderate** | Balanced risk/reward | Diversified portfolios, index funds |
| **Aggressive** | High risk, high growth | Growth stocks, crypto, startups |

### By Investment Goals

| Segment | Description | Email Strategy |
|---------|-------------|----------------|
| **Retirement** | Long-term wealth building | 401k, IRA, compound interest content |
| **Wealth Building** | Growing net worth | Investment strategies, portfolio tips |
| **Passive Income** | Regular income stream | Dividends, rental income, bonds |
| **Short-term Gains** | Quick returns | Trading tips, market opportunities |

### By Experience Level

| Segment | Description | Email Strategy |
|---------|-------------|----------------|
| **Beginner** | New to investing | Educational content, basics |
| **Intermediate** | Some experience | Strategy deep-dives, analysis |
| **Advanced** | Experienced investors | Advanced strategies, market insights |

### By Investment Timeline

| Segment | Description | Email Strategy |
|---------|-------------|----------------|
| **Short-term** | <1 year | Tactical opportunities, market timing |
| **Medium-term** | 1-5 years | Balanced approach, goal-based |
| **Long-term** | 5+ years | Strategic allocation, compound growth |

## Personalization Rules

| Data Point | Segmentation Rule | Email Personalization |
|------------|------------------|----------------------|
| Risk Tolerance | Conservative | Show low-risk investments first |
| Risk Tolerance | Aggressive | Show growth opportunities first |
| Investment Goal | Retirement | Include retirement calculators |
| Investment Goal | Passive Income | Include dividend recommendations |
| Experience | Beginner | Use simple language, link to guides |
| Experience | Advanced | Use technical analysis, detailed data |
| Timeline | Short-term | Focus on current opportunities |
| Timeline | Long-term | Focus on compound growth |
