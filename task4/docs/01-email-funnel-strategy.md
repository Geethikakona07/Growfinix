---

## Why Zero-Party Data?

| Data Type | Definition | Value |
|-----------|------------|-------|
| **Zero-Party Data** | Data customer intentionally shares | Highest value — explicit preferences |
| **First-Party Data** | Data you collect from interactions | High value — behavioral insights |
| **Third-Party Data** | Data from external sources | Lowest value — being phased out |

### Benefits of Zero-Party Data
- ✅ GDPR/CCPA compliant by design
- ✅ Higher accuracy (self-reported)
- ✅ Better personalization
- ✅ Increased trust and transparency
- ✅ Future-proof marketing

| Quiz | Timeline | Quiz Q4 | Prioritize leads |

## Value Exchange

### What Users Give
- Contact information
- Quiz responses (zero-party data)
- Engagement data (opens, clicks)

### What Users Get
- Personalized investment recommendations
- Free investment strategy guide
- Market insights and updates
- Priority access to opportunities
- Personalized newsletter content
