1. Executive Summary

This project delivers a comprehensive "Value-Exchange" Email Funnel for a Startup Investment Firm, designed to collect zero-party data through interactive quizzes and preference collection. With the phase-out of third-party cookies, this funnel establishes first-party data collection as the foundation for personalized marketing.

Key Deliverables:
• Complete email funnel strategy (5 stages)
• Lead magnet + landing page copy
• Welcome email + 3 follow-up emails
• 10-question investment quiz (Typeform)
• Segmentation strategy (4 segments)
• Mailchimp & HubSpot workflows
• 50 email subject lines

12. Interview Questions & Answers

#QuestionAnswer
1What is zero-party data?Data that customers intentionally and proactively share with a brand. It's explicit, self-reported information like preferences, interests, and intentions. Highest value because it's accurate and consent-based.
2Why is zero-party data important?With third-party cookies being phased out, brands need first-party and zero-party data. It's more accurate, GDPR/CCPA compliant, and enables better personalization.
3How do you collect zero-party data?Through interactive quizzes, surveys, preference centers, polls, and direct questions. The key is providing value in exchange for the data (value exchange).
4What is a value exchange?Providing something valuable (guide, recommendations, insights) in return for customer data. Both parties benefit: customer gets value, brand gets data.
5How do you segment email lists?By demographics, behavior, preferences, engagement level, and lifecycle stage. For investment: risk tolerance, goals, experience, timeline.
6What tools do you use for email marketing?Mailchimp or HubSpot for automation, Typeform for quizzes, Google Analytics for tracking, CRM for lead management.
7How do you measure email marketing success?Open rate, click-through rate, conversion rate, revenue attribution, unsubscribe rate, and customer lifetime value.
8What makes a good email sequence?Clear value proposition, personalization, progressive engagement, strong CTAs, mobile optimization, and testing/optimization.
