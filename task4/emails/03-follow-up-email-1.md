## Email Body

```
Hi [First Name],

Since you completed our investment quiz, we wanted to share 
3 common mistakes we see investors make — and how to avoid them.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Many investors put too much in one asset class. 
Our data shows diversified portfolios perform 
23% better over 5 years.

✅ What to do: Spread investments across stocks, 
bonds, and alternative assets.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

MISTAKE #2: Emotional Decision-Making

Fear and greed drive 70% of bad investment decisions.
The market rewards patience, not panic.

✅ What to do: Set clear rules and stick to them. 
Automate when possible.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

MISTAKE #3: Ignoring Fees

High fees can eat 30% of your returns over time.
Most investors don't realize how much they're paying.

✅ What to do: Compare expense ratios. Choose low-cost 
index funds when possible.

[BOOK A FREE CONSULTATION →]

Our advisors can review your portfolio and identify 
opportunities you might be missing.

Talk soon,
[Name]
[Firm Name]
```
