# 01 — Landing Page Optimization

## Medical Clinic — Page-by-Page Optimization Guide

---

## Homepage Optimization

### Title Tag (55-60 chars)
```
Best Medical Clinic in [City] | Primary Care | [Clinic Name]
```

### Meta Description (150-160 chars)
```
Trusted medical clinic in [City] with board-certified doctors. Primary care, preventive screenings, and specialist consultations. Book your appointment today.
```

### H1 Tag
```
Trusted Medical Clinic in [City] — Compassionate Care for Your Family
```

### H2 Structure
```
H2: Our Medical Services
├── H3: Primary Care
├── H3: Preventive Health
├── H3: Chronic Disease Management
└── H3: Specialist Consultations

H2: Why Choose [Clinic Name]?
├── H3: Board-Certified Physicians
├── H3: Modern Medical Facilities
├── H3: Patient-Centered Approach
└── H3: Convenient Location

H2: Meet Our Doctors
└── H3: [Doctor Names]

H2: What Our Patients Say
└── H3: Patient Reviews

H2: Frequently Asked Questions
└── H3: [Individual Questions]
```

### Content Requirements
| Element | Minimum | Optimal |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Word Count | 1,000 | 1,500 |
| Internal Links | 5 | 8 |
| External Links | 2 | 3 |
| Images | 5 | 8 |
| FAQs | 5 | 8 |
