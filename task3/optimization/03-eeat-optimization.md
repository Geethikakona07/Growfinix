# 03 — E-E-A-T Optimization

## Medical Clinic — Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness

---

## E-E-A-T Overview

| Signal | Definition | Medical Importance |
|--------|------------|-------------------|
| **Experience** | First-hand experience with topic | Patient outcomes, case studies |
| **Expertise** | Professional knowledge | Board certifications, education |
| **Authoritativeness** | Recognition by others | Medical associations, publications |
| **Trustworthiness** | Honesty and transparency | Reviews, accreditations, clear pricing |

## Experience Improvements

### Patient Success Stories

| Element | Implementation |
|---------|---------------|
| Case Studies | 5+ patient stories (anonymized) |
| Treatment Outcomes | Success rates and statistics |
| Before/After | Visual testimonials where appropriate |
| Video Testimonials | Patient video stories |

### Case Study Template

```markdown
# Patient Success Story: [Condition] Treatment

## Patient Profile
- Age: [Range]
- Condition: [Diagnosis]
- Duration: [How long before treatment]

## Challenge
- Symptoms experienced
- Impact on daily life
- Previous treatments tried

## Our Approach
- Initial diagnosis process
- Treatment plan developed
- Timeline of care

## Results
- Outcome achieved
- Patient testimonial
- Follow-up plan

## Key Takeaways
- What worked
- Lessons learned
- Advice for similar patients
```

## EEAT Content Guidelines

### Medical Content Rules

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Author byline** | Every article must have a named author |
| **Medical review** | Content reviewed by licensed physician |
| **Citations** | Reference medical authorities (Mayo Clinic, WHO, etc.) |
| **Last updated** | Show when content was last reviewed |
| **Original content** | Never copy from other medical sites |
| **Accuracy** | Fact-check all medical claims |

### Author Bio Template

```markdown
## About the Author

**Dr. [Name], [Credentials]**

Dr. [Name] is a board-certified [specialty] with [X] years of experience 
at [Clinic Name]. [He/She] specializes in [focus areas] and is passionate 
about [patient education/preventive care/etc.].

**Credentials:**
- MD, [Medical School]
- Board Certified, [Specialty]
- [X] years of clinical experience

**Connect:**
- [Clinic Profile Link]
- [LinkedIn] (if applicable)
```
