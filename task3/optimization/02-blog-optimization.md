## Blog Content Strategy

### Content Pillars
| Pillar | Description | % of Content |
|--------|-------------|--------------|
| Preventive Care | Health screenings, wellness tips | 30% |
| Condition Guides | Disease explanations, management | 25% |
| Treatment Information | Procedures, what to expect | 20% |
| Lifestyle & Wellness | Nutrition, exercise, mental health | 15% |
| Clinic News | Updates, events, announcements | 10% |

## Blog Post Template

### Standard Medical Blog Post

```markdown
# [Primary Keyword] — [Secondary Keyword] | [Clinic Name]

**Meta Title:** [Primary Keyword] | [Clinic Name]
**Meta Description:** [150-160 chars with keyword and CTA]
**Focus Keyword:** [primary keyword]
**Target Word Count:** 1,500-2,500 words

---

## Introduction (100-150 words)
- Hook the reader
- State the problem/question
- Preview what they'll learn
- Include primary keyword naturally

## H2: What is [Topic]?
- Clear definition
- Why it matters
- Statistics if relevant

## H2: Signs and Symptoms
- Bulleted list of symptoms
- When to see a doctor
- Risk factors

## H2: Diagnosis and Treatment
- How it's diagnosed
- Treatment options
- What to expect

## H2: Prevention and Management
- Lifestyle changes
- Prevention tips
- Management strategies

## H2: When to See a Doctor
- Red flags
- Urgency levels
- CTA to book appointment

## H2: Frequently Asked Questions
- 3-5 common questions
- Detailed answers (50-100 words each)

## H2: Book an Appointment
- CTA with contact info
- Link to booking page

---

**Medical References:**
1. [Medical Authority Source]
2. [Research Study]
3. [Medical Association Guidelines]

**Author:** Dr. [Name], [Specialty]
**Medically Reviewed By:** Dr. [Name], [Specialty]
**Last Updated:** [Date]
```

---

## Blog Post Checklist

### Pre-Publish
- [ ] Primary keyword in title
- [ ] Primary keyword in meta description
- [ ] Primary keyword in H1
- [ ] Secondary keywords in H2 tags
- [ ] Word count 1,500+ words
- [ ] Internal links (3-5)
- [ ] External links (2-3 medical authorities)
- [ ] Images with alt text
- [ ] FAQ section included
- [ ] Author bio added
- [ ] Medical references cited

### Technical SEO
- [ ] Yoast SEO score 80+
- [ ] Readability score 60+
- [ ] Schema markup (Article)
- [ ] Mobile-friendly formatting
- [ ] Fast loading images

### EEAT Elements
- [ ] Author credentials displayed
- [ ] Medical reviewer credited
- [ ] Last updated date shown
- [ ] Citations from medical authorities
- [ ] Original content (not copied)
