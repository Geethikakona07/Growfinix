16. Interview Questions & Answers

#QuestionAnswer
1What is E-E-A-T and why is it important for medical websites?E-E-A-T = Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness. Medical content is YMYL (Your Money or Your Life) — Google applies highest scrutiny. Strong EEAT ensures rankings and AI citations.
2What schemas are important for medical websites?MedicalClinic, Physician, FAQPage, Review, LocalBusiness, Article. These help search engines understand medical content and display rich results.
3How do you optimize content for medical SEO?Use target keywords naturally, structure with H1/H2/H3, add medical NLP terms, cite medical authorities, include FAQ sections, display doctor credentials, and maintain content freshness.
4What tools do you use for medical SEO?Screaming Frog (technical audit), Yoast SEO (on-page), Surfer SEO (content optimization), Google Search Console (performance), Schema Markup Validator (schemas).
5How do you establish trust for medical websites?Display doctor credentials, show patient reviews, add accreditations, implement HIPAA compliance, provide transparent pricing, and cite medical authorities.
6What is the difference between medical SEO and regular SEO?Medical SEO has higher YMYL standards, requires EEAT signals, needs medical schemas, must cite authoritative sources, and faces stricter content scrutiny.
7How do you optimize for AI search in healthcare?Create structured content with clear definitions, data tables, FAQ sections, expert quotes, medical citations, and comprehensive schema markup.
8How do you handle medical content guidelines?All content reviewed by licensed physicians, cite medical authorities (Mayo Clinic, WHO), display author credentials, show last updated dates, and never make unsupported claims.
