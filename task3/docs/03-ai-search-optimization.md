## GEO Optimization Strategy

### 1. Structured Data for Medical Content

| Schema Type | Purpose | Priority |
|-------------|---------|----------|
| MedicalClinic | Identify clinic to AI | Critical |
| Physician | Identify doctors | Critical |
| MedicalCondition | Define conditions treated | High |
| MedicalProcedure | Define procedures offered | High |
| FAQPage | Answer common questions | Critical |
| Review | Patient testimonials | High |
| Article | Blog content | High |

### 2. Content Structure for AI Citations

| Element | Best Practice |
|---------|--------------|
| **Clear H1** | Primary keyword + location |
| **H2 Sections** | Each condition/service clearly defined |
| **Data Tables** | Symptoms, treatments, pricing |
| **FAQ Sections** | 5-10 questions per page |
| **Expert Quotes** | Doctor insights in content |
| **Citations** | Reference medical authorities |

### 3. Medical Content Template

```
H1: [Service] in [City] | [Clinic Name]

Introduction (150 words)
- What is the condition/service
- Why it matters
- How the clinic helps

H2: What is [Condition/Service]?
- Definition
- Symptoms
- Risk factors

H2: Our [Service] Approach
- Treatment options
- What to expect
- Why choose us

H2: Meet Our Specialists
- Doctor credentials
- Experience
- Specializations

H2: Frequently Asked Questions
- 5-10 common questions with detailed answers

H2: Book an Appointment
- CTA with contact information
```

## Medical Content EEAT Requirements

| Signal | How to Implement |
|--------|-----------------|
| **Experience** | Case studies, patient stories, treatment outcomes |
| **Expertise** | Doctor credentials, medical education, certifications |
| **Authoritativeness** | Medical association memberships, publications |
| **Trustworthiness** | Reviews, accreditations, transparent pricing |
