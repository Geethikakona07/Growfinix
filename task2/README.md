## Executive Summary

This project delivers a comprehensive **30-Day Content Calendar** for a **Luxury Fashion Store** across Instagram and LinkedIn. The calendar includes AI-generated viral hooks, captions, Reel concepts, carousel ideas, story sequences, and Canva design suggestions — all optimized for maximum engagement and brand growth.

## How to Use This Calendar

1. Open the daily calendar for your platform
2. Follow the content type and topic for each day
3. Use the provided hook as your opening line
4. Copy and customize the caption
5. Follow the Canva design suggestions for visuals
6. Post at the recommended times
7. Use the suggested hashtags
8. Engage with comments within 1 hour of posting
