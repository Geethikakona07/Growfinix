## Hook Performance Tips

| Tip | Detail |
|-----|--------|
| **First 3 seconds** | The hook must appear visually AND audibly |
| **Text overlay** | Always display the hook as on-screen text |
| **Music sync** | Choose music that matches the hook's energy |
| **Pattern interrupt** | Use unexpected visuals to stop the scroll |
| **Face on camera** | Hooks with faces get 38% more engagement |
