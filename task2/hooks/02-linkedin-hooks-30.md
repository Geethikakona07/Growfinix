## LinkedIn Hook Best Practices

| Tip | Detail |
|-----|--------|
| **First line** | Must stop the scroll — this is the only visible text |
| **Use line breaks** | White space increases readability |
| **Be specific** | Numbers and details beat vague statements |
| **Ask questions** | Drives comments, boosts algorithm |
| **Be authentic** | Personal stories outperform corporate speak |
