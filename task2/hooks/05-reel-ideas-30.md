## Reel Production Checklist

- [ ] Hook appears in first 3 seconds
- [ ] Text overlay matches voiceover
- [ ] Music is trending and on-brand
- [ ] CTA is clear and actionable
- [ ] Video is 15-30 seconds optimal
- [ ] Captions/subtitles added
- [ ] Hashtags in caption (20-30)
- [ ] Tagged products (if applicable)
