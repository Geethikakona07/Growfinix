## Carousel Best Practices

| Tip | Detail |
|-----|--------|
| **Cover slide** | Must have a clear, compelling hook |
| **Consistency** | Same fonts, colors, and style throughout |
| **One idea per slide** | Don't overcrowd |
| **Readable text** | Large fonts, high contrast |
| **End with CTA** | Tell them what to do next |
| **Use numbers** | "5 ways...", "3 tips..." increase saves |
| **Save-worthy** | Make content people want to reference |
