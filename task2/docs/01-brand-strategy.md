## Content Pillars

### Instagram Content Pillars

| Pillar | Description | % of Content | Examples |
|--------|-------------|--------------|----------|
| 🛍️ **Product Showcase** | Highlight new arrivals, bestsellers, collections | 30% | Flat lays, styled outfits, detail shots |
| 🎬 **Behind the Scenes** | Show brand story, team, process | 20% | Warehouse tours, packing orders, design process |
| 💡 **Style Tips** | Educational fashion content | 25% | "How to style...", "5 ways to wear...", trend reports |
| 🤝 **Social Proof** | UGC, reviews, testimonials | 15% | Customer photos, unboxing, reviews |
| ✨ **Lifestyle & Inspiration** | Aspirational content | 10% | Mood boards, quotes, aesthetic imagery |

### LinkedIn Content Pillars

| Pillar | Description | % of Content | Examples |
|--------|-------------|--------------|----------|
| 📊 **Industry Insights** | Fashion industry trends, data | 30% | Market reports, trend analysis |
| 💼 **Business Story** | Brand journey, milestones | 25% | Founder story, growth updates |
| 🎓 **Education** | E-commerce, marketing tips | 25% | "What we learned...", business lessons |
| 🤝 **Team & Culture** | People behind the brand | 10% | Team spotlights, hiring posts |
| 🌟 **Thought Leadership** | opinions on industry | 10% | Hot takes, predictions |
