13. Interview Questions & Answers

#QuestionAnswer
1How do you create a content calendar?Start with brand goals and audience research. Define content pillars. Map content types to days. Align with posting times. Include hooks, captions, CTAs, and hashtags. Use AI tools for ideation.
2What makes a good Instagram hook?Creates curiosity, promises value, or provokes emotion. Must appear in first 3 seconds. Use numbers, questions, or bold statements. Text overlay reinforces the hook.
3How do you measure content performance?Track engagement rate, reach, saves, shares, profile visits, and website clicks. Compare week-over-week and month-over-month. Use platform analytics tools.
4What's the difference between Instagram and LinkedIn content?Instagram: visual-first, casual tone, entertainment value. LinkedIn: professional, data-driven, thought leadership. Same brand voice, different expression.
5How do you use AI for content creation?Generate hooks, captions, and ideas. Refine with brand voice. Use AI for brainstorming, not final copy. Always edit for authenticity and brand consistency.
6What's the ideal posting frequency?Instagram: 4-5 feed posts/week, 3-5 stories/day, 3-4 Reels/week. LinkedIn: 3-5 posts/week. Quality over quantity always.
7How do you create viral content?Focus on value, emotion, or entertainment. Use trending formats and sounds. Create shareable/saveable content. Engage with comments immediately after posting.
8What tools do you use for content creation?Canva (design), CapCut (video editing), ChatGPT/Claude (copywriting), Notion (planning), Later/Buffer (scheduling).
